# Student FAQ

**How do I connect to a college?** Type its approved name or abbreviation in a new chat. Existing conversations remember their college.

**Where do answers come from?** Each college answer uses its current stored facts and source pages. The badge shows whether evidence came from an official website, an admin entry, an upload, or general AI guidance.

**What if the bot says “Not available”?** The platform did not find verified evidence for that question. Use the college's official website or submit an enquiry when enabled.

**Can I upload a document?** Yes. Attach a PDF, DOCX, or image for conversation-only extraction. It is not added to college knowledge. Admins may upload approved circulars and documents into college data.

**How do I export or delete my data?** Open Settings and choose Download my data, Delete chat history, or Delete account.
